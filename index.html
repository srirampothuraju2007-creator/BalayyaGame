🎮 HOW TO PLAY

• Tap or Drag: Tap or drag anywhere on screen. Balayya automatically navigates around walls and crates using smart A* pathfinding.

• Wooden Crates [X]: Stand behind wooden crates to hide in the shadows. Police flashlight beams cannot pierce through crates!

• Stealth Takedowns: Sneak up behind any police officer to execute an instant mass takedown.

• Laser Tripwires: Avoid crossing red moving laser security beams to prevent sounding the alarm!

• Mansion House Bottles: Collect upright bottles dropped by defeated police or hidden in warehouse corners.
